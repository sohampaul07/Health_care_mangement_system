Federated AI Architecture & Distributed Learning

Privacy-preserving multi-facility model training. Local clinical telemetry remains strictly on edge devices while aggregated gradients build collective national intelligence.

PRIVACY-PRESERVING ARCHITECTURE PROTOCOL
Decentralized Machine Learning Without Data Movement

Patient privacy is protected by design: No electronic patient medical records leave any Primary Health Centre. Each participating edge node trains a localized logistic/regression model on local consumption registries, calculates parameter updates (gradients), adds differential privacy noise (ε=1.2, δ=10⁻⁵), and transmits only encrypted weights to the National Aggregator.
